Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding B1 English Daily, its educational materials, and its technology integration.

1. What is B1 English Daily?

B1 English Daily is an educational video production and publishing platform operated by Học Tiếng Anh Mỗi Ngày. It generates daily short-form videos designed to teach essential CEFR B1-level English vocabulary to learners on TikTok.

2. Who is the intended audience for these videos?

Our videos are tailored for English language learners worldwide who possess basic foundational English (A1/A2) and wish to advance toward intermediate proficiency (CEFR B1) through quick, engaging daily quiz practice.

3. Can external users sign up or create accounts?

No. B1 English Daily is currently maintained as an internal automation tool run solely by its operator to publish educational content to our channel. There are no external public user registrations, subscriptions, or paywalls.

4. How are videos published to TikTok?

Videos are published via TikTok's official developer Content Posting API. Our automation engine compiles the daily lesson, performs quality checks, and uploads the video directly to the operator's authenticated TikTok account.

5. Are the videos labeled as AI-generated?

Yes. In full compliance with TikTok Community Guidelines and transparency policies, any video produced with AI-assisted speech synthesis or rendering is explicitly marked with TikTok's AI-generated content label.

6. What vocabulary sources are utilized?

Our vocabulary lists are organized by CEFR level (A1–B1) and everyday topics. Each word's meaning and part of speech is checked against the Cambridge Dictionary before a video is published.

7. How can I contact the team?
