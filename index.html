B1 English Daily

Welcome to the official documentation and legal pages for B1 English Daily.

About the Application

B1 English Daily is an internal automation tool operated by an individual developer. It assists Vietnamese learners of English by generating and preparing daily short-form educational videos focused on B1-level vocabulary.

Target Audience

The resulting content is tailored for Vietnamese individuals seeking to improve their English language proficiency at the intermediate (CEFR B1) level.

How It Works

The application operates via automated workflows to create and publish educational materials:

  • Content Generation: Vocabulary lists and educational context are processed with AI assistance (Google Gemini API for text generation; AI synthesized voice; and stock video clips under free license from Pexels).
  • AI Disclosure: All published video content is explicitly tagged and disclosed as "AI-generated content".
  • Publishing Workflow: Videos are published strictly to the developer's personal TikTok account via official TikTok APIs or the intermediary service Zernio, upon explicit authorization by the developer.
  • No Public User System: The tool runs as a private backend automation pipeline controlled via a private Telegram bot interface. It has no public registration, user accounts, or viewer tracking mechanisms.
