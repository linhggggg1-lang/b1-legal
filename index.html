What is B1 English Daily?

B1 English Daily is a specialized content generation and publishing engine developed by Học Tiếng Anh Mỗi Ngày. Tailored specifically for language learners aiming for the CEFR B1 standard, the application automates the creation of bite-sized, interactive video lessons combining vocabulary definitions, audio pronunciation, contextual examples, and interactive recall quizzes.

Designed to be seamless and reliable, B1 English Daily bridges vocabulary databases with official TikTok Content Posting APIs to deliver consistent daily learning material directly to our followers.

Key Platform Features

Standardized B1 Curriculum

Vocabulary sets organized by CEFR level (A1–B1) and everyday topics, prioritizing essential daily verbs, phrasal verbs, idioms, and workplace phrases.

Interactive Quiz Formats

Engaging quiz structures that give viewers a count-down timer to test their recall before revealing the correct meaning and practical example sentence.

High-Fidelity Audio & Video

Crystal-clear synthetic narration paired with cinematic, royalty-free HD backgrounds for optimal visual retention on mobile feeds.

Official TikTok Integration

Automated direct scheduling and uploading using TikTok Content Posting API, ensuring prompt delivery at optimal learning hours.
