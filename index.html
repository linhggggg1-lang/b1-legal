Internal Operating Application

B1 English Daily is an internal automation tool operated by Học Tiếng Anh Mỗi Ngày. It is designed to automatically generate and publish daily short-form educational videos for learning B1-level English vocabulary.

Application Purpose & Scope

The application creates daily video lessons containing English vocabulary words, their definitions, phonetic transcriptions, spoken pronunciation, and video backgrounds. The rendered videos are published directly to the official TikTok account owned and managed by the operator (Học Tiếng Anh Mỗi Ngày).

Currently, B1 English Daily is maintained exclusively as a single-operator automation tool for our own TikTok channel. It does not provide public registration, external user accounts, or end-user software access.

AI Content Disclosure

Content generation within this application is assisted by artificial intelligence technologies, including AI-assisted text processing, synthetic voice generation, and curated background video footage. In compliance with TikTok Community Guidelines and Platform Policies, all published videos are prominently labeled as AI-generated content.
