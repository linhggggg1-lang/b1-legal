About B1 English Daily

B1 English Daily is an educational initiative managed and operated by Học Tiếng Anh Mỗi Ngày. Our mission is to make intermediate English vocabulary accessible, memorable, and fun for language learners through concise daily video quizzes.

Our Mission

Language acquisition thrives on consistent daily exposure. By synthesizing bite-sized video lessons that fit naturally into social media feeds, we help learners build long-term retention without feeling overwhelmed by lengthy grammar drills.
