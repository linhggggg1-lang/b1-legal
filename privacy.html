Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how technical information and data are handled within the B1 English Daily tool, operated by an individual developer (the individual developer, the developer's country of residence).

2. Data Collection and Usage

B1 English Daily does NOT collect personal data from public viewers, does NOT offer public user registration or user accounts, does NOT use tracking cookies, and does NOT sell any data.

The application only processes technical configuration and operational data strictly required for the developer's internal automated publishing workflow:

Data Category Purpose Storage Location
Vocabulary lists and configurations Storing educational curriculum and video production parameters Developer's private Google Sheets
Video media files Intermediate rendering and archiving of video assets Developer's private Google Drive
TikTok access tokens Authenticating video uploads to the developer's account Developer's private Google Sheets
API keys & secrets Authenticating automated CI/CD pipeline actions GitHub Actions Secrets (encrypted storage)
Control commands Triggering and monitoring generation workflows Developer's private Telegram bot interface

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The application integrates with the following third-party infrastructure and service providers solely to execute operational tasks:

  • Google (Google Sheets, Google Drive): Cloud storage for internal settings, tokens, and media files.
  • Google Gemini API: AI language model used to generate textual vocabulary explanations and context.
  • GitHub Actions: Cloud automation environment executing scheduled generation jobs.
  • TikTok API: Official platform endpoint for publishing authorized video content to the developer's channel.
  • Zernio: Intermediary automation service for scheduling and managing media distribution.
  • Pexels: Source for royalty-free, public stock background video footage under a free license.
  • Telegram: Private messaging bot API for sending administrative commands and status notifications.

4. Data Retention

Because no consumer or viewer personal data is collected, no end-user retention schedule applies. Operational credentials, tokens, and media assets are retained within private developer storage solely for as long as needed to maintain and operate the automated publishing schedule.

5. User and Subject Rights

Since the application does not collect, process, or maintain personal information or identifiers from general users or viewers, access, rectification, or deletion requests under privacy frameworks (such as GDPR or CCPA) do not apply to end-user personal records. Any general inquiries regarding public content or intellectual property may be submitted via our contact channel.

6. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly target, solicit, or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age limit in relevant jurisdictions). The application has no user input or account creation features.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
