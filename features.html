Platform Capabilities & Features

B1 English Daily combines automated language pedagogy with modern short-form video design to maximize vocabulary retention.

1. Curated CEFR B1 Vocabulary

Carefully filtered lexical sets focusing on the core CEFR B1 range (plus foundational A1/A2 reviews). Each word includes accurate part of speech, IPA phonetic transcription, concise definitions, and authentic example sentences.

2. Interactive Quiz & Countdown Mechanism

Each lesson incorporates visual prompt cards and timed countdown wheels that engage the viewer directly, prompting them to guess or recall the definition before the correct answer is revealed.

3. Multi-Speaker Audio Synthesis

Utilizing high-clarity neural speech engines to produce native-like English pronunciation, accompanied by natural pacing and clear phonetic modeling.

4. Dynamic Visual Typography

Rendered with bold, mobile-optimized typography, high-contrast layouts, and dynamic text highlights that ensure readability across all smartphone screen sizes.

5. Direct TikTok Content Posting API

Automated cloud publishing directly via TikTok's official developer API, scheduling posts at 08:00, 12:30, and 20:00 VN time to reach learners during routine commute and rest hours.

6. Transparent AI Disclosure

Full adherence to platform safety and developer standards: all AI-assisted elements are explicitly disclosed with TikTok's native AI-generated content labels.
